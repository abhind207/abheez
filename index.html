Our Story

Elegance Stitched Into Every Thread

At Abeeez Ladies Stitching Center, we have been the trusted name for premium ladies tailoring in Pulpally, Wayanad for over a decade. We believe every woman deserves a garment that fits not just her body, but her spirit.

From intricate bridal blouses to everyday churidars, our skilled team crafts each piece with meticulous attention to fit, finish, and fabric — because you deserve nothing less than perfect.
